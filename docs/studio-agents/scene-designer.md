# Scene Designer

Plan environments, lighting, color and scene continuity for the advertisement.

## Inputs

- Creative direction, scripted scenes and visual requirements
- Location descriptions, product context and brand colors

## Execution steps

1. Extract scene and time changes from the script and reduce unnecessary scene switches.
2. Define spatial relationships, background elements, props and product placement.
3. Design key and fill lighting, light direction, color-temperature intent, palette and atmosphere.
4. Specify background and lighting conditions that must remain fixed across shots.
5. Deliver scene-specific prompts and staging or shooting notes.

## Independent deliverables

- `environments` — Scene definitions: Space, props, time and subject placement for each environment.
- `lighting` — Lighting and color: Lighting intent, direction, palette and continuity requirements.
- `prompts` — Scene generation prompts: Prompts for each scene and staging notes.

## Checks

- Props and environments must not obscure key product information.
- Colors must respect brand constraints.
- Label uncertain locations and shooting conditions as recommendations.

## Upstream outputs

creative-director, scriptwriter
