## Creative workflow

Each template has an original Sparkle plan across Objective, Audience Insight,
Proposition, Big Idea / Concept, Narrative, Art Direction, and Execution & Conversion.
The cover and source link identify real brand campaign or promotional imagery;
these are not downloadable campaign films, licensed stock assets, brand-authored
briefs, endorsements, or claims about the actual campaign strategy or performance.
The Headspace cover is official app promotional imagery rather than a named film.
Style tags describe the editable Sparkle creative route, not necessarily the exact
technique used to produce the reference photograph. No brand imagery is sold by
these new free templates; the two existing paid workflows retain their demo-only
checkout behavior.

Using a template creates nine editable nodes: seven creative layers, the source
image reference, and an original opening-scene draft ready for the user's product and brand.
Nine directed connections carry insight and objective into proposition/concept,
then narrative and art direction into execution and the film draft. The reference
feeds art direction. The draft prompt includes all seven layers as context and asks for only the
opening five-second scene, consistent with the current generation controls. Existing project creation and canvas persistence APIs save the graph;
selecting a template does not initiate paid generation.

## Scenario coverage

| Commercial scenario | Templates / creative routes |
| --- | --- |
| Beauty, fragrance, jewelry & luxury | Golden presence; An everyday ritual — editorial, glamour, macro, soft minimal |
| Fashion, streetwear & sneakers | A new perspective — street culture, dynamic photography, Y2K, photomontage |
| Food & beverage | Pleasure in the detail — sensory macro, hyperreal, restrained pop-art color |
| Cars & technology hardware | The quiet product film; Sculpted in light — futuristic industrial, CGI, cinematic dark luxury |
| Home, appliances & lifestyle | Room to exhale — Scandinavian, warm lifestyle, architectural, soft minimal |
| Toys, children & games | Small world, big play — tactile stop-motion, playful cartoon and stylized 3D character |
| Commerce, FMCG & apps | One small reset — natural creator/UGC delivery, clear product demo, problem → solution, direct response |
| Sport & outdoors | Another way up — documentary, POV, slow motion, expansive landscape |
| Culture, music & art | The sound of belonging — analog, collage, mixed media, experimental film |
| Social good & brand image | Seen as you are — narrative, documentary, emotional storytelling, conceptual |

The visual bar is considered lighting, tactile materials, controlled color, strong
compositions and restrained typography. Even conversion-oriented templates retain
clear hierarchy, readable UI and credible demonstrations. No fake notifications,
random effects, unsupported product promises or fabricated performance numbers.
